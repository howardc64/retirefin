42. **Cost basis, unrealized gain, dividend waterfall; realized LTCG is now automatic (supersedes the LTCG modes in entries 14, 17, 31, 34 and 41).**
    - **Cost basis per portfolio** (new optional field under the balance; blank = not entered, which is different from `0`). Basis is tracked whenever one is entered or the portfolio's Expenses toggle is on (`pfBasisEntered()`, `pfTracksBasis()`); with Expenses on and a blank basis, it starts equal to the balance (no unrealized gain today). Average-cost method, all in today's $.
    - **Dividend waterfall.** Each portfolio's dividends (ODIV) pay that portfolio's own year's outflows (withdrawal + fee + tax drag) first; leftover dividends are reinvested and add basis; any shortfall is covered by selling shares, which removes basis in proportion to cost share and realizes the portfolio's gain share. An IDGT's dividends never pay another portfolio's expenses (still counted as household income for tax, as before). The balance itself is unchanged, because growth is total return.
    - **Realized LTCG is derived, not entered.** `LTCG = shortfall × gain fraction`, where `gain fraction = max(0, (value − basis) ÷ value)`, only when Expenses is on. The **Realized LTCG dropdown and its $ / % input are removed**, along with the `ltcg` / `ltcgMode` fields and the `onLtcgMode` handler (and the now-unused younger-household-age calculation). Tax drag in the shortfall estimate uses the prior year's Total Tax (same one-year lag as entry 41, so it stays non-circular); year 0 has no tax-drag term. Outflow logic (fee / tax drag / withdrawal) moved into one shared helper, `pfOutflows()`, used by both the LTCG estimate and the balance roll-forward.
    - **Basis roll-forward:** `basis = (basis − shortfall × (1 − gain fraction) + reinvested dividends) ÷ (1 + inflation)`. Basis erodes with inflation because it is a fixed nominal amount while the model runs in today's $, so gains and later LTCG grow in real terms.
    - **Step-up at death.** The year the owner has passed and the portfolio continues to a surviving spouse, a non-IDGT portfolio's basis resets to its value (unrealized gain → $0, no LTCG that year). IDGT portfolios keep carryover basis. Portfolios that don't continue leave the model at the owner's death, as before.

43. **Portfolio expenses are paid by household income first, then dividends, then sales (changes the payment order in entry 42; balances now change).**
    - **Payment order.** A portfolio's expenses (withdrawal + fee + tax drag, Expenses on) are paid (1) by the household's other income, (2) by that portfolio's own dividends, with any leftover reinvested (adds basis), and (3) by selling shares for any remaining shortfall, which triggers LTCG (`LTCG = shortfall × gain fraction`, as before).
