# Usage

## Additional Info

* **NOTES** Press Notes button to see supported/unsupported features. Common observations on retirement plans.
* **Formulas** Provide math book like formula showing the performed calculations

---

## Incomes

* **Pooling:** All incomes sources are pooled to pay expenses
* **Portfolio Income:** tax exempt income, dividend income are pooled to total income to pay for expenses
* **Portfilio Asset:** Is sold to cover shortfalls after using up all income sources
* **Unspend Incomes:** Unspent dividends reinvest back into their portfolio. Unspent other income invest into a main portfolio handling the main retirement living cost transactions (marked by checkbox)
* **Rental Depreciation:** Treated like untaxable real income because $ is actually made in rental operation but deducted for tax purposes.
* **IDGTs:** IDGT assumes grantor trust status and all income stays within portfolio

---

## Expenses

* **Paid by pooled Income:** All expenses are paid by pooled income (except IDGT income) These include living cost, taxes, IRMAA surcharge, AUM fees, LTC etc.
* **AUM Fees:** All portfolios and IRAs have AUM checkbox indication its part of the AUM. AUM fees are charged on the total AUM asset balance either as a % or fixed (not indexed to inflation or COLA). Fixed fees often can be negotiated once assets are structured by Financial Advisor and in lower work maintenance mode.

---

## LTC

* **Living Expense Change:** LTC completely change living expenses. For married couple, 1st person starting LTC usually still have the same living expense as before to support the 2nd person. When 2nd or single starts LTC, living expense usually becomes low or 0.
  
---

## IRAs

* **Roth Conversion:** Can set start age and conversion amount for pacing the conversion. After pre-tax IRA balance is exhausted, conversion stops.

---

## Portfolio

* **Delayed Start:** For assets that will shift into investment portfolios in the future. For example, stocks or real estate investments planning to liquidate in the future.

---

## User Interface Control

* **Enable checkbox:** each income source has an enable checkbox on the left upper corner. Enable triggers the input parameters to be used in projection calculations which is then displayed in charts on the right. If unchecked, input parameters will remain but not go into projections.
* **Hide checkbox:** collapse a section to reduce display screen usage. For income sections, input data still contributes to projections if enable is checked.

## Asset Value

* **Stretched** extended 10 year after least person passing to show for example IRA value (must be fully withdrawn 10 year after passing)
* **Devalue:** can devalue assets for analysis
  * unrealized gains by % of of unrealized gain (LTCG tax rate)
  * pre-tax IRA by % (ordinary income tax rate)
  * devaluing “values” the asset as if fully withdrawn and taxes paid. Can be used to compare with tax exempt assets such as Roth IRA; For example, deciding on Roth Conversion amount and pace.
  * Devalue % can be set before and after last person passing. Before represent tax bracket of owner and after represent tax bracket of the heir.
  * Devalue assets withdrawn as ordinary income > 0% turns off basis dash line since devaluing is like a fully withdraw and taxes paid (basis = value)
